### Two things here that are decisions, not errors

An independent review on 2026-09-02 raised both of these as defects. Both were then confirmed by
the site owner. Please do not "correct" either one.

- **The date "25 August 2026" appears nowhere inside the document.** Its text carries no date at
  all — the only date in ten pages is "in August or September 2026", a forward reference to
  guidance still to come — and the PDF metadata reads `CreationDate 2026-09-02`, which is when
  the Word file was exported, not when the template was written. The date comes from the filename
  supplied by the site owner, **who confirmed it as correct on 2026-09-02**. It is also baked into
  the public URL, so changing it would need a redirect, not a rename.

- **The PDF metadata names `Vitali Moiseenko` as the author**, not the working group. The
  collective attribution above is the site owner's decision, on the basis that the template is a
  group product and the metadata records whoever exported the file.
