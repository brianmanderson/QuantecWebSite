## The theme

Every edit in the document moves the site's vocabulary in one direction: away from words that
assert a limit the planner must respect (*constraints*, *dose limits*, *dose thresholds*) and
toward words that describe observed data (*toxicity risk metrics*, *dose-volume data*,
*dose-volume correlates*, *dose metrics*).

This is a clinical-framing change, not a cosmetic one. It aligns the site's language with what
the QUANTEC tables actually are — pooled outcome data with stated caveats — rather than
prescriptive planning limits. No dose value, paper title, author list or URL is altered by any
item below.

### Notes on individual items

**Item 1.** The tagline is `site.description` in `_config.yml`, not a literal in the layout. It
is therefore also the site-wide SEO meta description that `{% seo %}` emits on every page, so
this edit changes both the visible tagline and page metadata. `_config.yml` changes are not
picked up by `jekyll serve --watch` — the server must be restarted.

**Item 3.** The owner supplied the replacement sentence in full, so it is transcribed verbatim
rather than composed. Note it drops "and guidelines" and adds a purpose clause ("in determining
acceptable dose/volume planning objectives"). The current sentence wraps `evidence-based
radiation dose constraints` in `<span class="highlight">`; the replacement keeps that span around
the corresponding phrase.

**Item 9.** The document reads "Hearth dose constraints". The site says `Heart dose constraints`
(`quantec.html:49`) and the owner's own replacement text spells it "Heart", so this is a typo in
the quoted string, not a request to change a word that exists. Applied to the real string.

**Item 10.** This bullet's wording was itself an owner amendment on 2026-08-03 (see
[update-requests.md](update-requests.md), ruling 2 supersession). The new wording still names V20
and still does not name V30, so it remains consistent with the verified lung table, which carries
`V20 ≤ 30%` and five mean-dose rows and no V30 row.

**Items 11–13.** These three bullets are link text inside `<a>` elements pointing at
`/publications/#abdomen`. Only the visible text changes; the hrefs are untouched.

## Content-accuracy check

Every item is qualitative framing prose. **No item alters a dose value, a DVH cut-off, an NTCP
parameter, a paper title, an author list, a journal reference or a URL.** Items 8–13 weaken
rather than strengthen the claim each bullet makes (a "limit" becomes a "correlate"), which is
the safe direction of travel for this site.

One string must **not** be swept up by item 7: the paper title
"Radiation Associated Brainstem Injury" at `quantec.html:161` is a citation and is copied from
the source. It keeps the one-word spelling regardless of which spelling the prose adopts.

## Open questions for the owner

### Q1 — Does the nav tab "Constraints" get renamed, and to what?

image1 circles the nav tab as well as the tagline, and the caption says replace "constraints"
with "toxicity risk metrics". Read literally, the tab becomes **Toxicity Risk Metrics**.

The obstacle is physical. `assets/css/style.css:58-62` records the measurement: the six nav items
occupy 569px of a 594px budget at 1280px — 25px of slack — and the comment warns that at 605px
the nav drops to its own row and the header grows from 130px to 167px. "Constraints" (11
characters) → "Toxicity Risk Metrics" (21) adds roughly 75px. That is far outside the slack, so
the literal rename wraps the header on every page unless something else gives.

Options: rename in full and accept a taller header (or shorten another item to pay for it);
rename to something shorter that carries the same sense ("Risk Metrics", "Toxicity Metrics");
or read image1's nav circle as pointing only at the tagline beneath it and leave the tab alone.
The URL stays `/quantec/` in all cases.

### Q2 — How far does the terminology sweep reach?

The document quotes exact strings, and each mapped item above is one of them. But the same
vocabulary appears in seven places the document does not mention, and leaving them makes the
home page say "toxicity risk metrics" in the tagline while a button underneath says "Browse
Constraints":

| `index.html` | 98 | "A quick route from an organ at risk to its **constraint** paper" |
| `index.html` | 106 | "Every **constraint** on this site routes to the peer-reviewed review…" |

Sweeping these is consistent with the document's intent; not sweeping them is consistent with its
letter. The site's content-authority rule says the owner's words govern, which argues for asking
rather than assuming.

### Q3 — Which brain stem spelling?

The owner delegated the choice ("please pick one"). Two words, **"Brain stem"**, is proposed: it
is what the transcribed QUANTEC Table 1 source uses (see
