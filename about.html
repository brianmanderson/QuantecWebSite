About QUANTEC Radiation

Advancing radiation oncology through evidence-based toxicity risk metrics and clinical research.

Our History

The original QUANTEC (Quantitative Analyses of Normal Tissue Effects in the Clinic) project was a landmark initiative in radiation oncology. It summarized the three-dimensional dose/volume/outcome data available at the time in order to update and refine the normal tissue dose/volume tolerance guidelines set out in the classic Emami et al. paper of 1991.

Building upon this foundation, QUANTEC Radiation continues this important work by maintaining, updating, and expanding these critical resources for the modern era of radiation therapy.

Our Objectives

  • Provide up-to-date, evidence-based toxicity risk metrics
  • Facilitate research into normal tissue complication probability models
  • Support clinical decision-making through comprehensive guidelines
  • Foster collaboration between clinicians, physicists, and researchers
  • Advance the science of radiation therapy treatment planning
