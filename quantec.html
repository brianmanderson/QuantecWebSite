QUANTEC Dose Constraints

Organ-specific dose/volume topics covered by the QUANTEC reviews, each routing to the paper behind it.

How to use this page

The dose/volume tolerance guidance on this site comes from the original QUANTEC reviews, published as a special issue of the International Journal of Radiation Oncology Biology Physics. This page routes you to them: find the organ system, then follow it to the review that carries the constraint.

Dose/volume/outcome values are stated directly in the tables below for the central nervous system, head and neck, and thorax. They are transcribed from the QUANTEC summary table, with its authors' own caveats reproduced alongside them and every row attributed to the page it came from. Abdomen and pelvis are still being transcribed, and route to the paper in the meantime.

Dose/volume/outcome data

Spinal cord, lung and heart. Every row below is transcribed from a single source — Table 1 of the QUANTEC summary paper — and carries the page it appears on.

Read this first. The table these rows come from carries two footnotes from its authors. Both are reproduced verbatim, because they govern how every value below may be used.

All data are estimated from the literature summarized in the QUANTEC reviews unless otherwise noted. Clinically, these data should be applied with caution. Clinicians are strongly advised to use the individual QUANTEC articles to check the applicability of these limits to the clinical situation at hand. They largely do not reflect modern IMRT.
All at standard fractionation (i.e., 1.8–2.0 Gy per daily fraction) unless otherwise noted. Vx is the volume of the organ receiving ≥ x Gy. Dmax = Maximum radiation dose.

Source for both footnotes and every table below: Marks LB, Yorke ED, Jackson A, et al. “Use of Normal Tissue Complication Probability Models in the Clinic.” IJROBP 2010;76(3 Suppl):S10–S19, Table 1 (pp. S15–S18).
